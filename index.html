Fractions: add two fractions

Example: 1/4 + 1/2

Step 1 — Find a common denominator.
Use 4, because both 4 and 2 divide into 4.
Step 2 — Rewrite the fractions.
1/4 stays 1/4. Change 1/2 to 2/4 by multiplying the top and bottom by 2.
Step 3 — Add the numerators.
1/4 + 2/4 = 3/4. Keep the denominator the same.

Answer: 3/4

Remember: when denominators match, add the top numbers and keep the bottom number.

Percentages: find a percent of a number

Example: What is 15% of 80?

Step 1 — Turn the percent into a decimal.
15 ÷ 100 = 0.15.
Step 2 — Multiply by the number.
0.15 × 80 = 12.

Answer: 12

15% of 80 = 12

Equations: solve for x

Example: 3x + 5 = 20

Step 1 — Undo addition/subtraction first.
Subtract 5 from both sides: 3x = 15.
Step 2 — Undo multiplication.
Divide both sides by 3: x = 5.

Answer: x = 5

The key rule: do the same operation to both sides so the equation stays balanced.
